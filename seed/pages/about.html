This page came with the theme. It is ordinary content — open it in the admin and write what this site is actually about.

Everything you can see here is editable: the text you are reading, the page title, the address it lives at. Nothing on this page is special, and deleting it breaks nothing.

Why a starting page at all

A theme that ships only templates leaves you editing a template to change a sentence, which is the thing a content system exists to avoid. So the theme brings a page or two, and the platform is authoritative from then on — your edits are never overwritten by a later install, and a page you have changed is refused rather than replaced.

The front page is not seeded, on purpose: it stays the self-replacing first-run panel until somebody writes it.
