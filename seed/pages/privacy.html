This site has not published a privacy policy yet.

The page exists so the cookie notice and the footer have somewhere honest to point. It is ordinary content — the operator of this site replaces it with their own policy, which is not something software can write for them.

A generated policy would be worse than none: it would state commitments nobody made, about processing nobody has described, and a reader would have no way to tell it from a real one. So this says the true thing instead, and names who has to fix it.

If you are that operator: this page is at /privacy, under Content in the admin.
